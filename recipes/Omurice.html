Omurice Recipe

Description

Omurice is a japanese comfort food made of seasoned rice and a fried egg.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (day-old works best)
  • 150g chicken thigh, diced
  • 1/2 onion, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 tbsp ketchup, plus more for topping
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a pan and cook the chicken and onion until the chicken is done.
  2. Add the peas and carrots, then the rice, and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in the ketchup, salt and pepper until the rice is evenly coloured. Set it aside on a plate.
  4. Beat the eggs with the milk and a pinch of salt.
  5. Melt the remaining butter in a clean pan over medium-low heat and pour in the eggs.
  6. Stir gently until the eggs are half-set and still soft, then stop and let the base set.
  7. Spoon the rice onto one half, fold the omelette over it, and slide it onto a plate.
  8. Top with ketchup and serve warm.
